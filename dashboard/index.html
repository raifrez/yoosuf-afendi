    Journey rules

      The map outlines are real (OpenStreetMap). The islands, people and ferries in the demo network are fictional and placed in one atoll only to show how the tool works. Results show modeled residents without same-day access under these rules. They are not counts of patients, missed appointments or school absences. A missing return trip doesn't mean an overnight stay is available.
